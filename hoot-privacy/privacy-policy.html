Introduction

Hoot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address, name, and profile picture.
  • Username: The username you create in the app.
  • Friend Connections: Information about your friend relationships and friend requests.
  • Messages: Messages you send through the app (these are ephemeral and automatically deleted).
  • Device Information: Push notification tokens to deliver messages to your device.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your account and provide access to the app
  • Enable friend connections and friend requests
  • Send and receive messages between friends
  • Send push notifications for friend requests and messages
  • Improve our services and user experience

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase, a service provided by Google. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information.

Ephemeral Messages: Messages sent through Hoot are designed to be temporary. They are automatically deleted after you view them or after 24 hours, whichever comes first.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication. Google's privacy policy applies to sign-in data.
  • Firebase: For data storage and backend services. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Expo: For app development and push notifications.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications
  • Remove friends and manage your connections

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
