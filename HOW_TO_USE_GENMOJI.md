# How to Use Your Custom Genmoji in the App

Since Genmojis are images (not text emojis), you'll need to extract the image and use it as a picture file.

## Step 1: Extract the Genmoji from iMessage

### Method 1: Save from iMessage (Easiest)
1. Open the iMessage with your Genmoji
2. **Long press** on the Genmoji
3. Tap **"Save"** or **"Add to Photos"**
4. The image will be saved to your Photos app

### Method 2: Screenshot and Crop
1. Take a screenshot of the Genmoji in iMessage
2. Open Photos app
3. Edit the screenshot and crop just the Genmoji
4. Save the cropped image

### Method 3: AirDrop to Mac
1. Long press the Genmoji in iMessage
2. Tap **"Share"**
3. Select **"AirDrop"** and send to your Mac
4. Save the file

## Step 2: Transfer to Your Project

1. **Connect your iPhone to your Mac** (or use AirDrop/iCloud)
2. **Copy the Genmoji image** to your Mac
3. **Rename it** to something simple like `hoot-emoji.png`
4. **Place it in**: `/Users/clarkchung/Desktop/Hoot/assets/images/hoot-emoji.png`
